Jake

A private AI assistant that helps one person with everyday tasks.

Jake is a personal assistant built on OpenClaw. It runs on its owner's own computer and helps with reminders, planning, research and errands, mostly over chat.

Jake has its own Google account. With its owner's permission, it can read and send email, manage events and use files in that account only, through Google's official APIs.

Jake is not a public service: there are no sign-ups, and it is not offered to anyone else.
